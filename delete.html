勇者貓數獨 刪除資料申請

更新日期：2026 年 11 月 9 日

繁體中文English

本頁說明如何刪除 Android 遊戲「勇者貓數獨」（開發者：TIFFANY GAMES）保存在我們伺服器上的資料。遊戲不需要註冊帳號；伺服器上和你有關的資料只有「勇者試煉賽」的紀錄。

方法一：在遊戲內刪除（立即）

  1. 打開遊戲，進入「設定」。
  2. 點「試煉賽玩家代碼」。
  3. 點「刪除試煉賽資料」，閱讀說明後按「繼續」，再按「確定刪除」。

已登入 Google Play 遊戲時，按鈕是「刪除帳號與資料」：除了伺服器上的試煉賽紀錄，也會一併刪除和 Google Play 遊戲的綁定，以及這個遊戲的雲端存檔。你的 Google 帳號本身不受影響。

完成後，伺服器上的紀錄會立即刪除。下次參加試煉賽，會是新的玩家身份。

方法二：寄信申請（30 天內處理）

  1. 在遊戲「設定」找到你的玩家代碼（8 碼，例如 ABCD-EF23）。
  2. 寄信到 toybox0218@gmail.com，信中附上玩家代碼。
  3. 我們會在 30 天內刪除，並回信告知。

玩家代碼只用來找到要刪除的紀錄，不能用來登入，也不能用來操作你的紀錄。

找不到玩家代碼時（例如已經移除遊戲）：試煉賽紀錄會在該賽季結束 90 天後自動刪除，你不需要另外申請。

Cat Hero Sudoku Data Deletion

Updated: November 9, 2026

This page explains how to delete the data that the Android game Cat Hero Sudoku (developer: TIFFANY GAMES) keeps on our servers. The game needs no account; the only server data linked to you is your Hero Trial record.

Option 1: Delete in the game (immediately)

  1. Open the game and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Trial player code.
  3. Tap Delete trial data, read the notice, tap Continue, then Delete.

When you are signed in to Google Play Games, the button reads Delete account and data: besides your trial records on our server, it also removes the link to Google Play Games and this game's cloud save. Your Google account itself is not affected.

Your records are deleted from our server at once. If you join the trial again, you start as a new player.

Option 2: Request by email (handled within 30 days)

  1. Find your player code in the game's Settings (8 characters, for example ABCD-EF23).
  2. Email toybox0218@gmail.com with your player code.
  3. We delete your records within 30 days and reply to confirm.

The player code is used only to find the records to delete. It cannot be used to sign in or to change your records.

If you cannot find your player code (for example, you uninstalled the game): Hero Trial records are deleted automatically 90 days after the season ends. You do not need to send a request.
