勇者貓數獨 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 26 日

本政策說明 TIFFANY GAMES（以下稱「我們」）開發的 Android 遊戲「勇者貓數獨」（以下稱「本遊戲」）如何處理資料。

1. 我們不直接蒐集個人資料

  • 本遊戲不需要註冊或登入，也不會要求你提供姓名、電子郵件或電話。
  • 遊戲進度、設定與道具數量只儲存在你的裝置上。我們沒有架設伺服器接收或保存這些資料。
  • 移除本遊戲或清除應用程式資料後，這些資料會一併刪除。

2. 廣告與第三方服務

本遊戲使用 Google AdMob 顯示廣告。為了提供與衡量廣告、防止詐欺與濫用，Google 可能透過其 SDK 自動蒐集或接收下列資料：

  • 裝置識別碼，例如廣告 ID、應用程式組 ID
  • IP 位址，以及由 IP 推估的大致位置
  • 裝置與應用程式資訊，例如裝置型號、作業系統版本、語言、本遊戲版本
  • 廣告互動資料，例如廣告曝光與點擊

在法律允許且取得必要同意的情況下，Google 可能使用這些資料提供個人化廣告。這些資料由 Google 依其政策處理：

  • Google 隱私權政策
  • Google 如何使用採用其服務的網站或應用程式所提供的資訊

3. 你的選擇

  • 歐洲經濟區、英國與瑞士的玩家，第一次開啟本遊戲時會看到 Google 提供的同意視窗，之後可在遊戲設定的「隱私選項」修改。
  • 依你所在地區的法律，你可能有權拒絕將資料用於個人化廣告。適用地區會顯示對應的選擇或退出訊息。
  • 你也可以在 Android 系統設定中重設或刪除廣告 ID。

4. 兒童隱私

本遊戲的目標對象為 13 歲以上的玩家，並非以 13 歲以下的兒童為對象。我們不會在知情的情況下蒐集兒童的個人資料。如果你認為有兒童透過本遊戲提供了個人資料，請與我們聯絡，我們會協助處理。

5. 與我們聯絡時

如果你寄電子郵件給我們，我們只會用你的信箱與信件內容回覆、處理你的問題，不會用於其他目的，也不會出售或提供給他人。

6. 資料安全

我們不在伺服器保存玩家資料。本遊戲與 Google 廣告服務之間的資料傳輸使用加密連線。

7. 政策變更

如果本遊戲新增功能（例如雲端存檔、數據分析或付費項目）而改變資料處理方式，我們會更新本頁並修改生效日期。

Cat Hero Sudoku Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

This policy explains how TIFFANY GAMES ("we") handles data in the Android game 勇者貓數獨 / Cat Hero Sudoku (the "Game").

1. We do not directly collect personal information

  • The Game does not require registration or sign-in, and does not ask for your name, email address, or phone number.
  • Game progress, settings, and item counts are stored only on your device. We do not run servers that receive or keep this data.
  • Uninstalling the Game or clearing its app data deletes this data.

2. Advertising and third-party services

The Game shows ads through Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud and abuse, Google may automatically collect or receive the following through its SDK:

  • Device identifiers, such as the advertising ID and app set ID
  • IP address, and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information, such as device model, OS version, language, and Game version
  • Ad interaction data, such as impressions and clicks

Where permitted by law and with any required consent, Google may use this data to show personalized ads. Google processes this data under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

3. Your choices

  • Players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland will see a consent message provided by Google when first opening the Game, and can change their choice later under "Privacy options" in the Game's settings.
  • Depending on the laws where you live, you may have the right to opt out of the use of your data for personalized ads. Where applicable, the corresponding choice or opt-out message is shown.
  • You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android system settings.

4. Children's privacy

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us and we will help.

5. When you contact us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to and resolve your request. We do not use it for other purposes, and we do not sell or share it.

6. Security

We do not store player data on servers. Data sent between the Game and Google's ad services travels over encrypted connections.

7. Changes to this policy

If we add features that change how data is handled, such as cloud saves, analytics, or in-app purchases, we will update this page and its effective date.
