勇者貓數獨 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 3 日

繁體中文English

適用版本：本政策適用於目前的測試版本 1.0.0。勇者試煉賽在此版本尚未開放；開放時，我們會在上線前更新本政策。

本政策說明 TIFFANY GAMES（以下稱「我們」）開發的 Android 遊戲「勇者貓數獨」如何處理資料。

1. 不需要註冊

遊戲不需要註冊帳號。遊戲進度、星星、道具、設定，以及你的本機名牌和對戰紀錄，儲存在裝置上。清除應用程式資料或移除遊戲，會刪除這些本機資料。

2. 線上對戰

在勇者對決、怪物大逃殺或好友房中，遊戲會傳送你的名牌（從預設詞庫選取的名稱、四位數編號和頭像）、房間及連線資訊，以及翻格、判定、表情等對戰操作。其他參與者可以看到名牌及對戰公開資訊。遊戲沒有自由文字輸入或聊天。

好友房可能使用裝置之間的直接連線；此時對方裝置可能得知你的 IP 位址。快速配對與公開房間的對戰訊息由伺服器中繼，遊戲不向對手提供你的 IP 位址。

伺服器由 Cloudflare 提供。伺服器會處理來源 IP 與連線資訊，以建立連線和防止濫用。對戰訊息只轉送，不寫入持久儲存。伺服器暫存資料及保留規則如下：

資料用途與保留方式
房號、房間設定、座位、名牌、重連憑證雜湊供配對、加入及重連使用。房間關閉，或無人連線滿 10 分鐘後清空。
以密鑰 HMAC-SHA-256 處理的 IP 識別及錯誤房號計數防止大量猜測房號；限流紀錄在最後一次相關錯誤的 1 分鐘計算區間結束後清除。不在限流儲存中保留明文 IP。
匿名房間用量收據用於用量核對與避免重複計算；最後更新後 48 小時到期，於一般請求時分批清理。不含名牌、IP 或對戰內容。
每日用量摘要用於服務容量管理；保留最近 30 天，不含名牌或 IP。
Cloudflare 自身的網路及安全紀錄由 Cloudflare 依其隱私政策處理及保留。

3. 廣告與同意選項

遊戲使用 Google AdMob。Google 廣告 SDK 會收集及分享 IP 位址（可推估大致位置）、App 互動、診斷資料，以及廣告 ID、應用程式組 ID 等識別碼，用於廣告、分析和防止詐欺。依適用情況，也可能處理與裝置上已登入帳戶相關的其他識別碼。SDK 傳輸資料時使用 TLS 加密。

在適用地區（例如歐洲經濟區、英國與瑞士），遊戲會依 Google 的同意流程顯示所需訊息；需要提供隱私選項時，設定頁會顯示「隱私選項」，供你調整選擇。你也可以使用 Android 的廣告 ID 控制功能，重設或刪除廣告 ID。實際可用選項依系統及所在地而異。

詳情請參閱 Google 隱私權政策、Google 如何使用合作夥伴網站或應用程式的資訊，以及 Google 廣告 SDK 資料揭露說明。第三方資料依該服務的政策處理。

4. 兒童

本遊戲以 13 歲以上玩家為對象，不以兒童為目標對象。如果你認為兒童向我們提供了個人資料，請用下方信箱聯絡，我們會處理並刪除我們持有的相關資料。

5. 資料刪除

  • 本機資料：可透過 Android 清除應用程式資料，或移除遊戲來刪除。這會失去裝置上的遊戲進度。
  • 線上對戰：我們不保存對戰內容或建立永久玩家帳戶。房間暫存、限流紀錄及匿名用量資料依第 2 節自動清除或到期；因此離房不代表所有匿名用量摘要立即刪除。如對線上資料有疑問，可寄信聯絡我們。

6. 資料安全

遊戲使用加密連線與服務通訊；伺服器中繼使用 HTTPS／WSS，好友房直接連線使用 WebRTC 的傳輸加密。遊戲不要求提供姓名、電話或電子郵件。我們不出售你的個人資料。沒有任何傳輸或儲存方式能保證絕對安全。

7. 第三方服務

  • Google AdMob：廣告及同意訊息。
  • Cloudflare：配對、牽線與對戰中繼服務。

這些服務可能在你所在地以外處理資料，並依各自的隱私政策運作。

8. 聯絡時的資料

你主動寄信給我們時，我們會收到寄件地址及信件內容，僅用於回覆和處理你的問題。

9. 政策變更

功能或資料處理方式改變時，我們會更新本政策與生效日期。

Cat Hero Sudoku Privacy Policy

Applicable version: This policy applies to the current test version 1.0.0. Hero Trials is not yet available in this version; we will update this policy before it launches.

Effective date: November 3, 2026

This policy explains how TIFFANY GAMES (“we”, “us”) handles information in the Android game Cat Hero Sudoku.

1. No registration required

You do not need to register an account. Progress, stars, items, preferences, your local nameplate and battle history are stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game deletes this local data.

2. Online battles

In Hero Duel, Monster Royale and friend rooms, the game sends your nameplate (a name selected from preset words, a four-digit number and an avatar), room and connection information, and battle actions such as cell reveals, results and preset emotes. Other participants can see your nameplate and public match information. The game has no free-text entry or chat.

Friend rooms may use a direct device-to-device connection, which can expose your IP address to the other device. Quick Match and public rooms relay battle messages through the server; the game does not provide your IP address to opponents in these modes.

Cloudflare provides the servers. They process source IP addresses and connection information to connect players and prevent abuse. Battle messages are forwarded without being written to persistent storage. Temporary server data is handled as follows:

DataPurpose and retention
Room code, settings, seats, nameplates and hashed reconnection credentialsMatchmaking, joining and reconnecting. Cleared when the room closes or has no connected players for 10 minutes.
A keyed HMAC-SHA-256 IP identifier and failed room-code lookup countsLimits room-code guessing. Rate-limit records are cleared after the one-minute window following the last relevant failed lookup. Plain IP addresses are not retained in this rate-limit storage.
Anonymous room usage receiptsReconciles usage and avoids double counting. Expires 48 hours after its last update and is removed in batches during ordinary requests. Contains no nameplate, IP address or battle content.
Daily usage summariesService capacity management. Retained for the most recent 30 days, without nameplates or IP addresses.
Cloudflare's own network and security recordsHandled and retained by Cloudflare under its privacy policy.

3. Advertising and consent choices

The game uses Google AdMob. Google's advertising SDK collects and shares IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), app interactions, diagnostics and identifiers such as advertising IDs and app set IDs for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Where applicable, other identifiers related to signed-in device accounts may be processed. SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS.

Where applicable, including the EEA, UK and Switzerland, the game displays messages through Google's consent process. When privacy options are required, a “Privacy options” entry appears in Settings so you can adjust your choices. Android's ad ID controls also allow you to reset or delete your advertising ID. Available options depend on your system and location.

See Google's Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from partner sites and apps, and Google's advertising SDK data disclosure. Third-party data is handled under the relevant service's policies.

4. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and above and is not directed at children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact the email address below. We will address the request and delete relevant information we hold.

5. Deleting data

  • Local data: Clear the app's data through Android or uninstall the game. This removes progress stored on the device.
  • Online battles: We do not retain battle content or create permanent player accounts. Temporary room data, rate-limit records and anonymous usage data are cleared or expire under section 2; leaving a room does not immediately delete all anonymous usage summaries. Contact us with questions about online data.

6. Security

The game uses encrypted connections: HTTPS/WSS for server relay and WebRTC transport encryption for direct friend-room connections. The game does not ask for your name, telephone number or email address. We do not sell your personal information. No transmission or storage method can guarantee absolute security.

7. Third-party services

  • Google AdMob: advertising and consent messages.
  • Cloudflare: matchmaking, signaling and battle relay.

These services may process information outside your location and operate under their own privacy policies.

8. Contact information you provide

If you email us, we receive your email address and message. We use them only to respond to and handle your inquiry.

9. Policy changes

We will update this policy and its effective date when features or data handling practices change.
